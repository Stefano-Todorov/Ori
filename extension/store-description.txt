Orianna — AI Content Coach for Short-Form Video Creators

Save inspiration, research competitors, and generate video ideas while browsing TikTok and Instagram — all connected to your Orianna dashboard.

HOW IT WORKS

Browse TikTok or Instagram like you normally do. When you find a video worth saving, click the Orianna extension to:

• Save it as inspiration with custom tags
• See full post stats (views, likes, comments, engagement rate)
• Generate a video idea adapted to your niche using AI
• Analyze why a post performed well
• Track the creator as a competitor
• Open the original post with one click

FEATURES

Save Inspiration
One click to save any TikTok or Instagram video to your Orianna inspo feed. Add tags to organize by topic, format, or campaign.

Competitor Research
Orianna auto-detects when you're browsing a tracked competitor. Visit their profile to sort their posts by views, likes, or comments and find their top performers.

AI-Powered Ideas
Generate video ideas inspired by posts you find. Orianna's AI knows your niche, style, and past content — so ideas are tailored to you, not generic.

Post Analysis
Understand why a video went viral. Get an AI breakdown of what made the hook, format, and content work.

Bulk Import
On TikTok favorites or Instagram saved posts? Import them all to Orianna in one click.

Create from Inspo
Turn any saved video into a concrete content idea with hooks, angles, and CTAs — right from the extension.

REQUIREMENTS

Orianna does not track your browsing activity. Data is only collected when you actively use the extension. No third-party analytics. Read our full privacy policy at ori-nine.vercel.app/legal/privacy.
