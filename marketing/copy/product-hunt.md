## Description

### The Problem
Content creators spend more time planning than creating. Brainstorming ideas, researching what's working, writing scripts, scheduling posts — it's exhausting. Most creators burn out not from filming, but from everything that happens before they hit record.

### The Solution
Orianna is an AI content coach that handles the planning so you can focus on creating.

**AI Coach** — Chat with an AI that knows your niche, past scripts, and competitors. Get strategy advice that's actually personalized — not generic tips from a blog.

**Script Generator** — Turn any idea into a ready-to-film script with hooks, body sections, CTAs, and hashtags. Get multiple variants and pick your favorite.

**Ideas Board** — A visual kanban for every video idea. Track each one from concept through recording, editing, and posting.

**Competitor Research** — Track any creator across TikTok and Instagram. Import their top posts, see engagement metrics, and generate ideas adapted to your style.

**Chrome Extension** — Save any trending video to your swipe file while you browse. One click captures the video with full metrics. Then turn it into your own content idea from the dashboard.

**Content Calendar** — Plan recording days, assign ideas to dates, and see your production pipeline on one screen.

### Who It's For
Short-form video creators on TikTok and Instagram who want to post consistently without burning out. Whether you're just starting or you have 100K followers — if you make short-form content, Orianna is built for you.

### Pricing
Free. No credit card required. No usage limits.
