## Tips for Best Results

1. **Always point Pomelli at your live site first** — it extracts your brand DNA (colors, fonts, tone)
2. **Use Photoshoot** for product mockups — upload a screenshot of your dashboard
3. **Use Animate** to turn any static ad into a video ad
4. **Download at highest resolution** and resize as needed
5. **Generate 3-5 variations** of each prompt and pick the best
